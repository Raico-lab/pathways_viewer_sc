# Translation glossary (Japanese → English)

Use these terms consistently in the UI, help, manual, and data translations. English UI text uses sentence case
("Filter by name", not "Filter By Name"). Gene names stay as they are (TOR1, SCH9); protein complex names as in
Complex Portal / papers (TORC1, PKA). Keep units/symbols (→ ⊣ ◇ ▸ ▾ log2, p < 0.05).
